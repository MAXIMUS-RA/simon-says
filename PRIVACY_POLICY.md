# Privacy Policy

**Project:** Simon Says  
**Author:** Максим Рацілевич  
**Last updated:** April 2026  

---

## 1. General Information

Simon Says is an educational browser-based game developed as part of a university course project (КОП).  
This application does **not** collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers.  
All data is stored **locally** in your browser only.

---

## 2. What Data We Use

| Data Type | Purpose | Storage Location | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| `simon_says_gdpr_consent` cookie | Remembering your cookie consent choice | Browser cookie | 365 days |
| Game settings (background color, difficulty) | Saving your preferences | `localStorage` | Until manually cleared |
| Game results (scores, history) | Displaying your results history | `localStorage` | Until manually cleared |

---

## 3. Cookies

This application uses **one cookie**:

- **Name:** `simon_says_gdpr_consent`
- **Purpose:** Stores whether you have accepted or declined optional cookies (GDPR requirement)
- **Type:** Functional (required for consent management)
- **Expires:** 365 days

We do **not** use:
- Tracking or advertising cookies
- Analytics cookies (Google Analytics, etc.)
- Third-party cookies

---

## 4. localStorage

The application uses browser `localStorage` to save:
- Your selected background color
- Game difficulty settings
- Game results and history

This data **never leaves your device** and is not accessible to anyone other than you.

---

## 5. Your Rights Under GDPR

As a user covered by the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to:

- **Access** — view all data stored locally via browser DevTools (`Application → localStorage`)
- **Delete** — clear all data at any time via browser settings (`Clear site data`)
- **Withdraw consent** — decline cookies via the cookie banner at any time
- **Data portability** — all data is already local and fully under your control

---

## 6. Third-Party Services

This application does **not** integrate any third-party services such as:
- Analytics platforms
- Advertising networks
- Social media widgets
- External APIs that collect user data

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

This application is suitable for all ages. No personal data of minors is collected.
